No. 01

Atelier

Modernism made edible

There is no menu at Atelier. No choices, no printed card — just a modernist tasting experience that changes constantly and executes at a level that would be remarkable in any city. Chef Marc Lepine's two-time Canadian Culinary Championship title and the restaurant's place on national and global lists have made it a standard-bearer for modernist dining in Canada. A dinner as art, and as theatre.

Visit Website

No. 02

Antheia

Fermentation as philosophy

Chef Briana Kim's fermentation-driven chef's counter is one of the most distinctive dining experiences in Canada right now, not just Ottawa. Sixteen seats, an open kitchen, and a menu that changes with Antheia's own fermentation calendar. Intimate, considered, and building a reputation that extends well beyond the city.

Reserve a Table

No. 03

Riviera

Grand dining, reborn

Set in a former bank on Sparks Street, Riviera is Ottawa's most dramatic dining room. Soaring ceilings, marble, and brass create a backdrop that feels both timeless and cinematic. The menu blends French technique with Canadian sensibility, offering dishes that are polished, confident, and built for the room they're served in.

Visit Website

No. 04

Stofa

Seasonal cooking, thoughtfully composed

Stofa is a study in balance: Canadian ingredients guided by global influences, plated with quiet precision. Every course feels considered — flavors layered, textures measured, and details handled with restraint. The room mirrors the cooking: modern, understated, and welcoming without excess.

Visit Website

No. 05

Beckta

Ottawa's fine dining institution

Beckta has long been a cornerstone of Ottawa dining — formal service, a deep cellar, and plates that lean classic more than cutting edge. It remains a fixture for those seeking the city's most established fine dining room, offering consistency, polish, and a sense of occasion that few can match.

Visit Website

No. 06

Coconut Lagoon

South India, elevated

Chef Joe Thottungal brings the flavors of Kerala into an Ottawa fine dining setting. The menu is rooted in South Indian tradition, refined through tasting menus and elegant presentation. A restaurant that balances spice and subtlety, heritage and polish — offering something rare and essential in the capital's dining scene.

Visit Website

No. 07

E18hteen

A Market mainstay

For years, E18hteen has anchored the ByWard Market with a menu of polished steak and seafood in a striking heritage space. High ceilings, exposed stone, and formal service shape the atmosphere. The cooking leans classic, delivered with consistency and confidence — a reminder of why E18hteen continues to hold its place in Ottawa's fine dining landscape.

Visit Website

No. 08

Socá

Latin roots, modern polish

Socá brings a contemporary lens to Latin-inspired cooking, marrying bold flavors with refined presentation. The room is sleek and lively, pairing elegance with energy. A place where Ottawa's fine dining feels global, without losing its sense of intimacy and care.

Visit Website

No. 09

Raphaël

Peruvian elegance on Elgin

Raphaël brings a refined expression of Peruvian cuisine to Elgin Street. From ceviche to carefully grilled meats, the menu highlights bold flavors through a polished, modern lens. An intimate room with thoughtful service, offering something distinctive in Ottawa's fine dining landscape.

Visit Website

No. 10

Fauna

Inventive, rooted, alive

Inventive Canadian cooking with bold flavors and a neighbourhood feel. Fauna has been one of Ottawa's most consistent and creative kitchens for years, drawing on local farms and producers to build a menu that feels both rooted and exciting. The room is warm, the cooking is confident, and the whole experience rewards those paying attention.
